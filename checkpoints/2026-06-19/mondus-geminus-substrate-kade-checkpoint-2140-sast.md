## Major species adapter locks

### Vampires

- Sunlight kills.
- Oak stakes turn them to ash.
- No biological procreation.
- No supernatural hybrids.
- Lineage through turning/conversion.

### Wolves / Lycans

- All shift at will.
- Full moon enhances, does not force.
- Immediate danger lets the wolf take command.
- Lycans outrank werewolves and alphas.
- Moon Goddess chosen mates are law.
- Rejection only under extremity.
- Can only impregnate true mates.
- Silver, wolfsbane, severe trauma are near-fatal.
- Lycan rarity: 1 per 100 pups.
- Alpha is heart; Luna is pulse.

### Witches / Sorcerers / Mages

- Interface and balance class.
- Not boxed by fixed ceiling.
- Peaceful/humble by nature because they feel creation directly.
- Among most respected beings beneath gods/angels/demons/demigods.
- Mapped by source, lineage, domain, training, cost, consequence, responsibility.

### Fae / Elves

- Fae and elves are separate.
- Elves rank over fae.
- Each court has king, queen, grand mage, grand elf, 2 elven ministers, 2 fae ministers.
- Fae plug into bargain/loophole/court/glamour law.
- Elves plug into elder-blood/order/lineage law.

### Ocean-Born

- Merpeople and sirens belong to Ocean-Born order.
- Can bond with humans.
- Fated mates gifted by Poseidon.
- Shift at will.
- Unsupported land-form limit: 72 hours.
- Pregnancy may be carried on land or ocean.
- Birth must happen on land.
- Child earth-bound until 16 normal / 12 royal.
- Child must return to ocean every full moon cycle.
- Witch guardian may be appointed with stipend and house.
- Atlantis is not the entire ocean; each ocean has its own civilization.
